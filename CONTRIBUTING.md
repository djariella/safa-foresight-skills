# Feedback and contributions

These skills are version 1.0. We expect them to have gaps, and we would like to hear about them.

## Telling us what happened

Open an issue. It helps if you say:

- which skill you were using and what you asked Claude to do
- what you expected and what you got
- the message, if a program failed
- your country, topic and group size, if the method did not fit

If you ran a workshop with the skills, open an issue called "Used in ..." and tell us how it went, including what you changed.

## Proposing a change

Open an issue that says what you would change and why. If you have edited a skill, attach the changed files or paste the new text. We will fold agreed changes into the next version and credit you in the change log.

Keep instructions in plain language, and keep the reasons next to the rules. A facilitator with no background in the method should be able to read a `SKILL.md` and follow it.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed in the same way as the rest of the repository: CC BY 4.0 for text and Apache 2.0 for programs.
